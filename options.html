        Privacy

        • PageProof runs only when you click Scan This Page, and only in that tab.
        • Readable page text is sent to the grammar service selected above, split into small chunks. Identical blocks are sent once.
        • Form fields, password fields, editable regions, code blocks and hidden content are never read.
        • Page text is never stored. Only your settings, dictionary and ignore lists are saved, in this browser.
        • See LanguageTool’s privacy policy at languagetool.org/legal/privacy.
